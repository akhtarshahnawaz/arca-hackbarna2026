## Design Context

### Users
A ~50-year-old municipal fire coordinator at a desk during an incident. They open ARCA to see which farms, care homes, and households run out of time first, choose stay-or-leave, and approve a phone call. They are not a GIS analyst and will not read a manual.

### Brand Personality
Calm, official, glanceable. Like a laminated desk card, not a startup dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light operations console. Large type, large targets, one accent (urgency red/amber). No decorative chrome. Anti-references: equal-button toolbars, uppercase micro-labels, invented phone formats, left-stripe “admin” rows.

### Design Principles
1. One job per view: decide this place, then call if that decision needs a call.
2. Words match the coordinator’s mouth: stay inside, leave, watch, hold — not latent / E.164 / ensemble.
3. Incomplete work is the loudest thing on the screen.
4. Familiar patterns only: labeled phone field, map zoom, sequential Call then Approve.
5. The product decodes codes, clocks, and errors. The user never reconciles two messages.
